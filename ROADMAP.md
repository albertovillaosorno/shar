  - **Dates:** 2026-07-14
  - **Progress:** Complete
- **Phase:** 2 — Generate the minor-unit manifest
  - **Dates:** 2026-07-14
  - **Progress:** Complete
- **Phase:** 3 — Classify minor units into deterministic packages
  - **Dates:** 2026-07-14 to 2026-07-15

  - **Dates:** 2026-07-16 to 2026-07-22; resumed 2026-08-02

  - **Dates:** 2026-07-14 to 2026-07-18
  - **Progress:** Complete

  - **Dates:** Started 2026-07-19

- **Phase:** 7 — Implement the faithful Unreal runtime
  - **Dates:** Started 2026-07-19
  - **Progress:** In progress. Runtime foundations exist; complete original
    gameplay remains.
- **Phase:** 8 — Verify fidelity, platforms, graphics settings, and performance
  - **Dates:** After Phase 7
  - **Progress:** Planned. Settings may scale performance but must not redesign
    base content.
- **Phase:** 9 — Add drop-in mods and AI-agent skills
  - **Dates:** After the faithful base game works
  - **Progress:** Planned. Mods own optional replacements and creative changes.
- **Phase:** 10 — Package validated native builds
  - **Dates:** After Phases 8 and 9
  - **Progress:** Planned.
- **Phase:** 11 — Complete playthrough verification and close the roadmap
  - **Dates:** Final phase
  - **Progress:** Planned.
